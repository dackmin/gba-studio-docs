# Map Collisions

Map collisions determine how the player will be able to navigate within the scene. See them as
walls, ground or obstacles.

Every scene type is grid-based (except `logos`), so collisions are defined for each individual tile.

<img src="/images/collisions.png" alt="Map Collisions" className="rounded-xl" height={400} />

<br /><br />

:::info
This topic does not cover actor-to-actor-specific collisions. See
[Collision Groups](/docs/content/collision-groups) for more information.
:::

<br /><br />

<img src="/images/collisions-toolbar.png" alt="Collision Types" className="rounded-xl" height={60} />

<br /><br />

There are 9 different types of collisions:
- **Blocks**: Prevent the player from moving through them, acting as walls or obstacles.
- **Left & Right Slopes**: Allow the player to walk up or down inclined surfaces.
- **Pass-through blocks**: Allow the player to jump through them from a direction but stand on
them from the opposite direction.
- **Pass-through slopes**: Allow the player to walk up or down inclined surfaces while still being
able to pass through them from certain directions.

:::info
Blocking tiles are represented on the scene preview as transparent red rectangles or slopes, while
pass-through tiles are transparent green.
:::


:::tip
When using the Collision Painting tool, use the left click to paint collisions and the right click
to erase them.
:::

<br />

If you are too deep into collision painting and not satisfied with the result, you can erase
everything using the `Select` tool, right-clicking the scene and choosing `Reset Collisions`.
